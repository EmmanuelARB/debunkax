# Possible evolutions

Deliberately left out of the first version.

- **Verdict feedback**: a "contest" button (flag the Claim, re-check with another model) or a "report" button (pre-filled email the user sends).
- **Sharing Verdicts**: as text or image cards. Left out because a shared "FALSE" stripped of its sources is too easy to misuse.
- **Google Play distribution**: the first version is distributed as a direct APK download only.
- **Older phones**: phones below Android 12 or 6 GB of RAM would need a cloud speech-to-text path.
